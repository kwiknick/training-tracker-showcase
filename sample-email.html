Why Speed Legs, not Mountain Legs, today: Mountain Legs builds climbing endurance and long-effort strength. Speed Legs targets the hamstrings, glutes, and hips that fail at high power output, the muscles Mountain Legs alone does not cover. The plan rotates both: Speed Legs twice a week, Mountain Legs once or twice a week.

Progression: run every exercise unweighted first. Add a light dumbbell once form holds, then move to a kettlebell over time. Step-ups stay unweighted; body weight alone is enough stimulus.
